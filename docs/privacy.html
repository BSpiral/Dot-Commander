Privacy Policy

Dot Commander: Pirates — Effective date: September 13, 2026

This page describes how Dot Commander: Pirates ("the game", "we", "our") handles information. We built the game to be playable without creating an account, and this policy explains what that means in practice.

Information we collect directly

We do not directly collect your name, email address, or any other contact information through the game. There is no account creation, sign-in, or in-game messaging.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, and purchase entitlement status (such as whether Remove Ads has been purchased) are stored locally on your device. This local data is not transmitted to us and is not linked to your identity.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show a banner advertisement and optional, voluntary rewarded video advertisements (used to open Common Chests). Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, general location, diagnostic, and usage information in order to serve and measure these ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies. Rewarded Common Chests are entirely optional — the game never requires watching an ad to make progress.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services (AdMob)

Purchases

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — Google Play handles the transaction directly, in accordance with its own privacy policy. Purchase status is used only to determine whether ads should be shown on your device.

  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

No cryptocurrency, NFTs, or real-money trading

Dot Commander: Pirates does not involve cryptocurrency, NFTs, cash rewards, or any form of player-to-player real-money trading. In-game currencies (coins, gems) and items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, cashed out, or transferred between players.

Children's privacy

Dot Commander: Pirates is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Beyond Google AdMob and Google Play Billing described above, the game does not integrate any other third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published at this same URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the Dot Commander: Pirates Google Play Store listing.
